## 4. Contradictions, gaps and unclear figures (for editorial decision)

The full list with severities is in `content/issue-001.js > flags`, and it appears in each prototype's review mode (`?review`).

**Critical**
- **F1:** Embassy: the contents page and headline say **Denmark**, but the body text says **Embassy of Canada** three times.
- **F13:** The Liuka Gold Mine article **ends mid-sentence**. It also has no byline and no date.

**High**
- **F2:** The sales article sets progress "during the 2026/2027 financial year", yet the pesticide sales are "by June 2026".
- **F3:** The annual sales figures sum to ≈18.8 bn. That matches *domestic* sales (18.79), not the 19.50 bn cumulative total. The annual series may be domestic-only.
- **F5:** The subhead says the "distribution network expands across the country", but councils served **fell** from 178 to 149.
- **F8:** The malaria prevalence figures are unsourced and undated.
- **F16 / F17:** No captions or photo credits anywhere. Most photos are 1,000–1,700 px wide, below print quality at large sizes.

**Medium / low:** F4, F6, F7, F9–F12, F14, F15, F18–F24, F26 cover product-name variants, the company-name variant ("Product" vs "Products"), "Magazine" vs "Newsletter", the "Chief Editor" vs "Editor" heading, the byline spelling of Aisha Mohamed/Mohammed, an unattributed Nanenane quote, typos, stray overset text in margins (PDF pp. 5 and 7), the pink page tint, the logo available only as a raster, missing event dates, and the QR destination.
