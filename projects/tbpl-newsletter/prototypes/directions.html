Three visual directions

Blue #066AB5 Yellow #FDC902 Green #009236 Sampled from Issue 001 artwork; logo used as supplied

All three keep the approved TBPL logo and brand colours unchanged. They differ in typography, how much space the colour takes up, and what leads each page: photography, data, or brand colour. Each one is shown as a cover sketch and a data page. Direction A is recommended, and the prototype pages are built in it.

Direction ARecommended

Documentary Ledger

A serious magazine about science and development. Warm uncoated paper, a confident serif masthead, and photographs given room. Brand blue carries authority, yellow marks data, and green marks biology. The pages read like reporting, not promotion.

Direction BData-first

Laboratory Report

The precision of an annual report. A white page, a visible 12-column grid, hairline rules and one sans family. Large light numerals open each section, and photographs sit in grid cells as evidence. It is the most systematic option and the easiest to keep consistent across future issues.

Direction CBrand-forward

Signal

This direction builds on the energy of Issue 001's blue-and-yellow masthead and makes it more disciplined. Saturated colour fields and a heavy grotesque in capitals give it a poster-like rhythm. It has the strongest shelf presence and continues most clearly from the first issue.
