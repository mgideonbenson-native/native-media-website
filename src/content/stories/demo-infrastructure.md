> This is a demonstration story. It shows how a real Native Media story will be laid out. It contains no real reporting, facts or figures.

Real stories open with a clear lead paragraph that tells the reader what the story is about and why it matters. Body text is set for comfortable reading, with subheadings, photography, data graphics, pull quotes and video where the story needs them.

## How a story section looks

Each section builds the story step by step, with context that helps a reader who is new to the subject. Claims are tied to sources listed at the end of the story, and the label above the headline tells the reader whether they are reading reporting, research-based analysis, opinion, an institutional statement or sponsored content.

## What goes here in a real story

Interviews, evidence, history and analysis, written in Native Media’s editorial voice and checked before publication.
