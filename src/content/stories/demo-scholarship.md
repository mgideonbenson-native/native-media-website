> This is a demonstration story. It shows how a real scholarship story will be laid out. It describes no real scholarship.

A story of opportunity explains what the opportunity is, who it is for and why it matters, then gives the practical details clearly: who offers it, who can apply, the deadline, and where to apply.

## What goes here in a real story

A short, accurate description of the scholarship, what it covers, how to apply and what makes a strong application, with a link to the official source.
