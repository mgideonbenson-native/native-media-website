In this episode of African Intelligence, we sat down with serial entrepreneur and venture advocate Henri Nyakarundi to challenge one of the biggest blind spots in African business today: treating innovation as a procurement expense rather than a capital-allocation strategy.

Henri breaks down why global corporate giants grow their valuations by taking strategic equity in emerging technologies. At the same time, too many African boardrooms still ask startups for “free pilots,” price cuts, and 90-day payment terms.

We explore how foreign investors routinely capture the value of African innovation, why corporate valuations across the continent remain stagnant, and what a pragmatic, high-impact corporate venture framework looks like in practice.
