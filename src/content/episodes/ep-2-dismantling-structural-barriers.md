In this episode of African Intelligence, we speak with Adedoyin Jaiyesimi, Chartered PR Practitioner, Co-Founder of The Comms Avenue, and author of the report Impact Beyond Borders.

Drawing from her research and experience developing communications talent across Africa, Adedoyin examines what needs to change if Africa's next generation of communicators is to compete, and lead, on the global stage.

The conversation goes beyond individual career advancement to ask a bigger question: who will build the communications talent and narrative infrastructure Africa needs to tell its own economic, business, innovation and development stories to the world?

## Why are we having this conversation?

Africa cannot fully shape the global narrative around its businesses, economies, innovation and development without investing in the people responsible for communicating those stories.

Building globally competitive African communications professionals is therefore more than a talent-development issue. It is part of building the infrastructure through which Africa defines, communicates and ultimately owns its narrative.
