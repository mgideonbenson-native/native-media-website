In this episode of African Intelligence, we speak with Taha Jiwaji, Founder and CEO of Beem, the Pan-African customer engagement and communications technology company operating across more than 25 African countries.

Drawing from his experience building communications and data infrastructure for businesses across the continent, Taha examines a simple but pressing question: what does it take for African businesses to build customer experience engines that actually drive growth?

## Why are we having this conversation?

Africa's growth story will be written by businesses that understand their customers deeply and serve them well. Building the systems, data and technology behind great customer experience is therefore more than an operational question. It is part of building the infrastructure through which African businesses compete, grow and tell their own story.
